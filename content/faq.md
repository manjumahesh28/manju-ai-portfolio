## Has Manju built anything with AI, LLMs or RAG?
Yes. This website's "Talk to Manju" assistant is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that Manju built. Her CV, project write-ups and this FAQ are split into chunks, turned into embeddings with Gemini, and stored in a vector index. When a visitor asks a question, the most relevant chunks are found by cosine similarity and given to the language model, which is told to answer only from them and to say when it doesn't know. She has also built deep-learning projects for skin disease prediction and bone fracture detection.
